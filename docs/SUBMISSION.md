## Overview

RelayResolve resolves Across transfer disputes on the Ethereum-to-Base route. GenLayer validators independently retrieve deposits, bridge records and settlement receipts, agree on exact chain facts, and finalize delivery or refund decisions while unresolved cases remain open. A second consensus workflow interprets commit-pinned incident and recovery documents, verifies their SHA-256 and cited quotations, and records whether a reported status is superseded, consistent, contradictory or inconclusive. The dashboard shows receipts, decision hashes, provenance and transaction progress. Live StudioNet evidence includes a real 295.322271 USDT refund, a real 4.951764 USDC delivery, a recovery replay and an authoritative protocol-policy interpretation. The replay's failed interface label is synthetic and clearly marked. Submitted labels remain caller assertions. The contract records decisions without moving funds.

## Evidence qualifications

The recovery replay uses real deposit/refund receipts and a synthetic earlier failed interface observation. There is no verified historical Across UI screenshot for that label. A separate policy review demonstrates an actual version-pinned Across publication. A generic-policy interpretation that validators rejected is included as failed-consensus evidence.
